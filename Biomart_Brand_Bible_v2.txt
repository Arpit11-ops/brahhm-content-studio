**4. BRAND VOICE ****&**** TONE**

**Tone Descriptors**

• Trustworthy — Evidence-backed claims: certifications, lab tests, sourcing details

• Warm — Speaks like a knowledgeable friend who cares about family health

• Grounded — Rooted in Indian tradition and ancestral food wisdom

• Clear — Simple language, no jargon, no complicated nutritional terminology

• Purposeful — Every word serves the mission: purity, honesty, mindful living

**Words We Use**

Pure / certified / lab-tested / chemical-free / natural / wholesome / farm-fresh / deliberately-chosen / curated / essentials / restock / family-first / verified / investment / bundle / all under one roof / trusted since 2003 / conscious pantry / smart choice

**Words We Avoid**

Overused wellness buzzwords without substance / clinical pharmaceutical language / preachy eco-lectures / complicated jargon / rushed or impulsive energy / hard-sell tactics

**Tone by Format**

• Product page: Informative, honest, ingredient-forward

• Social post: Warm, conversational, educational. Never hard-sell.

• Festival: Celebratory, family-oriented, gift-giving focus

• Offer/promo: Clear value. Code + threshold always present.

• Customer support: Empathetic, prompt, solution-first
