**1. BRAND FOUNDATION**

**Origin Story**

Health Fields is the original flagship brand of Brahhm Arpan Organic Pvt. Ltd., established in 2003 — over two decades of organic food experience. It holds fully organic status per APEDA standards, certified by USOCA (APEDA-accredited), and is available across major retailers and online marketplaces across India.

**Mission Statement**

By providing high-quality, farm-fresh organic goods, to add value to customers' expectations — making certified organic food accessible, trustworthy, and nourishing for every Indian family.

Primary reel archetypes:
1. Morning wellness routine — real person, real kitchen, warm light, tea preparation (reference: Chamomile reel 16.4K views)

3. Tea preparation ASMR — close-up brewing, pouring, steam rising

Reel format: 20-30 seconds. Warm golden light. Face visible for wellness moments. Teal undertone maintained.

1. Teal lifestyle wellness moments (reel thumbnails — T58)
2. Premium product hero shots (GPT Image 2.0 — T2, T48 family)

**Thumbnail Style:** T58 — Wellness Moment. Warm light. Cup/product in hands. Teal world.

Rozana sub-brand warmth should expand to more of the account. Health Fields can be authoritative AND warm when talking about morning rituals, daily habits, family wellness. The clinical edge serves ATTACK and EDUCATE. The warmth serves RELATE.
