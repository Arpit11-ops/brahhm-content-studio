**1. BRAND FOUNDATION**

**Brand Name ****&**** Meaning**

"Green" = fresh, natural, wholesome ingredients. "Dipz" = dips, sauces, condiments. Together: a fresh, fun, flavour-forward sauce brand that brings restaurant-quality taste home using real ingredients.

**Mission Statement**

To make every home cook a culinary adventurer — delivering restaurant-style sauces and gravies made with real ingredients, zero artificial additives, and the authentic flavours of Indian, Italian, Mexican, and Asian cuisines.

**Core Values**

• Bold Flavour — Sauces that actually taste like the real thing

• Real Ingredients — Ripe tomatoes, fresh garlic, real spices. No filler, no shortcuts.

• Clean Label — No artificial preservatives, colours, or flavours

• Culinary Adventure — Inspiring home cooks to explore global cuisines

• Convenience — Restaurant-style results in minutes, without the skills

**Logo Rules**

• Speech-bubble / leaf hybrid shape. "greendipz" in bold rounded white lowercase.

• Three red petal leaves at top — organic, natural ingredient origin symbol.

• Blue water drop below text — represents sauce, dips, fluidity.

• Always on white or very light green backgrounds.

• Minimum clear space: equal to height of the "g" on all four sides.

**Firefly AI Visual Direction for greendipz**

greendipz requires sauce ACTION in AI-generated images — the product must be shown with motion and texture. Two primary prompt styles:

• INDIAN GRAVIES PROMPT STYLE: Thick, rich pour of sauce onto dark slate surface. Finished dish visible in warm background (blurred). Dramatic overhead lighting. Props: fresh tomatoes, garlic cloves, whole spices. Deep reds and creams dominate.

• INDO-CHINESE SAUCE PROMPT STYLE: Dynamic splash/drizzle of sauce from above. Vibrant vegetables (capsicum, spring onion, mushroom) caught in motion. High-saturation. Background: dark wok-inspired surface. Chili Red + Vivid Green colour contrast.

Canva Overlay for greendipz action shots: 60% frame height, placed next to the pour/splash. Product jar slightly tilted at 15° for energy.

**Photography Style**

• Vibrant, high-saturation food photography — colours should pop like the logo

• Sauce hero shots: being poured, spread, or dolloped — motion and texture are key

• Finished dish shots: the meal greendipz made possible — plated, garnished
