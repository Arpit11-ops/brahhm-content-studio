HOOK RULES — RESEARCH-VALIDATED JUNE 2026:
1. First line must be under 8 words. Forces the "Read More" click
2. 50% of viewers drop off in first 3 seconds — the hook IS the content
3. Vulnerable first-person hooks outperform product-first hooks by 10-50x
   (14.4M views "Life's been a little chaotic" vs 1.3K views "Presenting the all-new")
4. Hooks that trigger DM shares are weighted highest by the algorithm
5. Face in frame increases retention by 35%
6. Hashtags now REDUCE reach by 23% vs no-hashtag posts — use max 3

HOOK HIERARCHY (from research, in order of virality):
  TIER 1 — Vulnerable confession / relatable struggle (9-14M views)
  TIER 2 — Identity statement / aspiration (900K-3M views)
  TIER 3 — Universal moment + product weave (250K-700K views)
  TIER 4 — Founder/origin story (100K-1M views)
  TIER 5 — Situation urgency / recipe reveal (50K-250K views)
  TIER 6 — Product announcement (1K-15K views — avoid as primary)
