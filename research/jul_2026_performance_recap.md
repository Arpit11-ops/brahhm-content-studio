### The one thing that worked
**Pusht "whole moong journey" reel** — 90-second transparency piece on how moong gets from farm to pack. 14.6K plays, 27 comments, 141 likes. **50–100× the reach of every other post we ran in July.** Every other reel/post landed at 3–10 likes and <300 plays.

It also matches the Bilona ghee reel by tales_of_gujarat (556K plays) and Whole Truth's "3.5 lakh kg dates de-pitted by hand" reel (218K plays).

### What went wrong
1. **Two brands did 89% of the posting.** Biomart + greendipz absorbed all output. Caveman, Health Fields, and Pusht (new handle) posted zero direct content. Those three represent premium ARPU per follower but were starved for a full month.
2. **Zero creator or celeb collabs.** Every single winning competitor reel in July was either celeb-fronted (Chings × Ranveer, Veeba × Rhea, Vahdam × Protean Stories) or personal-POV creator content. We ran none.
3. **Reels too short and too product-forward.** Our reels averaged 20s and led with the pack. Winning competitor reels averaged 40–90s and led with a person, a myth, or a process — pack revealed mid-reel or in the CTA card.
4. **No character, no series.** Veeba is running a multi-chapter Rhea Chakraborty "hot seat" series (Ch 1 + Ch 2 both cleared 340K+ plays). Whole Truth has "Uncle Shashank vs Next Gen" as a recurring character. Neither of these ideas needs a celebrity — just a returning face and format.
