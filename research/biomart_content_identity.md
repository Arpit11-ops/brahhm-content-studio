# Biomart Content Identity — June 2026
## The Strategic Problem + Research-Validated Solution

---

## THE PROBLEM

Biomart is the only account in the portfolio losing followers.
- 2,090 followers, -0.7% growth (44 follows, 58 unfollows)
- 68,072 views, 86.3% organic — strong reach but ZERO conversion
- Best performing content on the grid: reposts from Pusht and greendipz
- No independent content identity
- Spending on ads while losing followers = strategic failure

## RESEARCH FINDING

No successful marketplace-only Instagram account exists in the competitor data.
@twobrothersorganicfarmsindia (402K followers) succeeds because it's farm story + products, not "buy everything here."
@organicindiaofficial (91K) succeeds with lifestyle wellness content, not catalog posts.

Marketplace = discovery destination. It is NOT a content identity.

## THE IDENTITY: THE CURATION VOICE

Biomart becomes **the person who picked everything on the shelf** — not the shelf itself.

The positioning: "I tested 50 organic honeys. These 3 made the cut."

### What This Means In Practice

Biomart is NOT:
- A product catalog
- A repost account for house brands
- A deal/offer announcement channel
- A generic "organic is good" educator

Biomart IS:
- The trusted curator who does the homework so you don't have to
- The voice that says "I compared these, here's what's actually worth your money"
- The single-stop convenience play backed by verification authority
- The founder/team face that explains WHY this product made it to the store

### Content Pillars (3 only)

**PILLAR 1 — CURATION (45% of content)**
"I tested X organic products. These Y made the cut."
Format: Reel — real person reviewing, comparing, selecting products.
Face visible. Real opinions. Not scripted.
Why it works: This is inherently EDUCATE + RELATE. Saves and shares.

**PILLAR 2 — HAUL / BUNDLE (30% of content)**
"This week's organic haul — one order, certified everything."
Format: Reel — overhead shot of products being unpacked on counter.
ASMR sounds of packaging. Quick cuts. Price NOT mentioned.
Why it works: Aspirational. "I want that pantry." Saves.

**PILLAR 3 — BEHIND THE VERIFICATION (25% of content)**
"How we decide what makes it to biomart.in"
Format: Reel — team/founder explaining certification check process.
Warehouse walkthrough. Product arriving. Label being read.
Why it works: Transparency builds trust. Founder content drives follows.

### Reel Formula (Biomart-specific)

**BEAT 1 — THE QUESTION (0-3 seconds)**
"How do you know which organic brand to trust?"
"I tested 12 organic ghees. Watch what happened."
"Your grocery app has an organic filter. But certified by whom?"

**BEAT 2 — THE CURATION (3-15 seconds)**
Show the comparison. Real products on table. Reading labels.
Picking up. Putting down. Building to the selection.

**BEAT 3 — THE PICK (15-22 seconds)**
"These made the cut." Show the selected 2-3 products.
Quick beauty shot of each. Label visible.

**BEAT 4 — THE SHELF (22-25 seconds)**
"All available at biomart.in. One order."
Quick shot of the biomart.in website or packaged order.

### Content Angle Ratios
35% EDUCATE | 30% RELATE | 25% ATTACK | 10% CONVERT

### Grid Rules
Max 2 thumbnail styles:
1. Curated product spreads (flat-lay, clean, organized — T60)
2. Founder/team face moments (warm, trustworthy — real photos)

### What To Stop Immediately
1. Reposting content from other brand accounts — this IS the identity problem
2. Generic festival posts that could be any brand
3. Product catalog posts with no editorial voice
4. Deal/offer posts without context
5. Any content that doesn't answer "Why should I trust Biomart specifically?"

### The North Star
Biomart should feel like following a knowledgeable friend who happens to run
an organic store. Not like following a store.
