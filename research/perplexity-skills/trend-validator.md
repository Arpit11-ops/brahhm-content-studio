# Trend Validator — Indian Organic Food

## Auto-start on load
Go straight to Step 1. No preamble.

## Step 1. Get the angle or trend to validate

Ask: What do you want to validate? Examples:
- An ingredient (e.g. "ragi", "ashwagandha", "cold-pressed mustard oil")
- A content angle (e.g. "Label Padhega India transparency post", "farm-to-table origin story")
- A claim (e.g. "no preservatives", "traditionally stone-ground")
- A cultural moment (e.g. "World Environment Day", "monsoon season cooking")
- A competitor move (e.g. "Two Brothers just launched X — should we respond?")

## Step 2. Run validation searches

Search across these dimensions:

### Momentum Check
Search: "[topic] India 2026", "[topic] trending India", "[topic] Instagram India"
Question: Is this gaining momentum, plateaued, or declining?

### Saturation Check
Search: "[topic] organic brand India campaign", "[topic] D2C India content"
Question: How many brands are already using this angle? Is it oversaturated?

### Risk Check
Search: "[topic] FSSAI", "[topic] controversy India", "[ingredient/claim] banned India", "Label Padhega India [topic]"
Question: Any regulatory risk, consumer backlash, or misinformation associated with this?

### Consumer Sentiment Check
Search: "[topic] India consumer opinion", "[topic] review India Reddit", "[product] India feedback"
Question: What are real Indian consumers saying about this topic right now?

## Step 3. Validation verdict

Output a structured verdict:

**TOPIC:** [what was validated]

[2-3 sentences of evidence]

**SATURATION:** High / Medium / Low
[Which brands are using this angle, if any]

**RISK LEVEL:** Clean / Caution / Avoid
[Any FSSAI, regulatory, or reputational risk found]

**CONSUMER SENTIMENT:** Positive / Mixed / Negative
[What real people are saying]

**VERDICT:** Go / Modify / Avoid
[One sentence recommendation]

**IF GO — Best angle for each relevant brand:**

## Rules
- Research only — no captions, no copy
- Every verdict point must be backed by a findable source
- If no data found, say "insufficient data" — do not fill gaps with assumptions
- FSSAI and Label Padhega India checks are mandatory for any ingredient or claim validation
