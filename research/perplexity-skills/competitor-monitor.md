# Competitor Monitor — Brahhm Brand Intelligence

## Auto-start on load
Go straight to Step 1. No preamble.

## Step 1. Get the scope

Ask: Which competitor category do you want to monitor?
- Millet & Snacks (Caveman Organic rivals): @slurrpfarm, @yogabars.in, @earlyfoods, @thewholetruthfoods
- Organic & Farm (Health Fields / Pusht / Biomart rivals): @twobrothersorganicfarmsindia, @organicindiaofficial, @24mantrain
- Sauces & Gravies (greendipz rivals): @chingssecret, @veeba_in, @wingreensfarms
- Tea & Wellness (Health Fields rivals): @vahdamindia, @organicindiaofficial, @bluetea.in
- All categories

## Step 2. Research each account

For each competitor in the selected category, search:
- "[handle] Instagram 2026" — recent posts and campaigns
- "[brand name] India campaign" — any press or marketing coverage
- "[brand name] new launch" — product or content launches

Extract for each competitor:
- What content format they're using most (reels, carousels, static)
- What angles they're running (educational, attack, relatable, promotional)
- Any hooks or opening lines that are gaining traction
- Any new products, campaigns, or collaborations spotted
- Estimated engagement signal (high buzz / moderate / low)

## Step 3. Output competitive intelligence table

| Competitor | Recent Move | Format Used | Angle | Hook Pattern | Engagement Signal | Gap We Can Exploit |
|-----------|------------|-------------|-------|-------------|------------------|-------------------|

## Step 4. Strategic summary

After the table, write 3-5 bullet points:
- What the category leader is doing that others aren't
- Any angle that is oversaturated across all competitors (avoid)
- Any angle that no competitor is owning (opportunity)
- Specific recommendation for which Brahhm brand should respond and how

## Rules
- Research only — no captions, no copy
- Clearly attribute every finding to a specific competitor
- If a competitor has no recent findable activity, say so — do not invent
- Focus on actionable intelligence, not vanity metrics
