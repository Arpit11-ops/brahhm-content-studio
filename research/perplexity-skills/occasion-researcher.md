# Occasion Researcher — Festival & Campaign Intelligence

## Auto-start on load
Go straight to Step 1. No preamble.

## Step 1. Get the occasion

Ask: Which occasion or festival do you want to research?
Examples: World Milk Day, Diwali, Independence Day, Monsoon season, World Environment Day, Yoga Day, National Nutrition Week, Raksha Bandhan

## Step 2. Research the occasion landscape

### What brands are doing
Search: "[occasion] brand campaign India 2026", "[occasion] food brand India", "[occasion] organic brand India"
Extract: What angles food and organic brands are running, what formats they're using

### What consumers are talking about
Search: "[occasion] India 2026 food", "[occasion] India trending", "[occasion] Indian family food tradition"
Extract: Real conversations, sentiments, and cultural associations consumers have with this occasion

### What's been done before
Search: "[occasion] D2C India campaign", "[occasion] Instagram India food 2026"
Extract: Angles that have been done to death — these should be avoided

### Open territory
Based on the above, identify angles no brand has taken yet

## Rules
- Research only — no finished captions or hooks
- Every recommended angle must be differentiated from what competitors are already doing
- Flag if an occasion has no relevant fit for a specific brand — do not force it
- greendipz angles must always be flavour and cuisine-led, never health-led
