4. **Reel share holds at 40% attention-weighted** (17 reels — same as Oct and Sept). Reel days = Tue / Fri / Sat / Sun default. Every Diwali-week festival that lands on a default reel day BECOMES that day's reel (Nov 1, 6, 7, 8, 10, 15).

6. **No new nano-creator collabs this month.** November is a Puran-watched commerce month — keep execution tight, no third-party dependencies on launch week.
7. **December calendar seed.** Last week of Nov (Nov 24–30) plants seeds for December (winter wellness, year-end gifting, Christmas-adjacent Caveman cookie carousels).
