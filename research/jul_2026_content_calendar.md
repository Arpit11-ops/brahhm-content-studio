## STRATEGIC FRAME

### Why this calendar looks different from June
June was product-locked — every slot named one SKU on Day 0. July flips it: **angle first, product last.** Reason — July is culturally rich (Guru Purnima, Sawan, Teej, Nag Panchami, World Nature Conservation Day, Global Tiger Day, World Chocolate Day) and Brahhm's full catalog can flex into every one of those moments. Locking the SKU early throws away that flex.

### Competitor performance signals shaping this calendar
Pulled from `research/studio_intelligence.db` (10 benchmark accounts, 200+ posts):

| Signal | What the data says | What we do about it |
|---|---|---|
| **Reels carry reach** | yogabars.in avg **956K views/reel**, slurrpfarm **491K**. Image posts get near-zero views. | Reels reserved for highest-impact festival/cultural days |
| **Comment-bait giveaway** | thewholetruthfoods → **5,404 comments** with "Comment X for recipes + lucky winners get box" | One giveaway-Reel per brand in July (5 total inside the 15-Reel cap) |
| **Emotional creator monologue** | slurrpfarm "Mumma's out for work…" + yogabars "Life's been chaotic…" → millions of views | RELATE-Reel format for at least 6 of 15 reels |
| **Educational save-bait** | organicindia "25 years authentic, not just in name" + transparency manifestos = strong save share | EDUCATE-carousels do the saves heavy lifting (no reel needed) |
| **Hashtag whitespace** | Top competitor tags: #d2cindia #cleanlabelindia #milletsnacks #healthysnacking — Sawan/Teej/Conservation tags barely used | Brahhm rides festival tags in July as a discovery moat |

### Hard constraints (locked)
- **Reels cap = 15 across all 5 brands** (3 per brand max).
- **Posts + carousels are flexible** — scale by capacity, no fixed count.
- **Stories run daily** as standard (no slot count needed).
- No discount codes, prices, or offers in any output unless Puran instructs in session.
- Product slot is **left flexible** — angle dictates, product fills.

### Angle distribution (research-calibrated)
| Angle | % | Slots | Role |
|---|---|---|---|

| RELATE | 30% | ~27 slots | Reach, comments, follows |

### KPI mapping per content type
- **Reels** → reach + views + followers (15 reels = the reach engine)
- **Carousels** → saves + shares (EDUCATE-heavy, the trust engine)
- **Single posts** → comments + stoppers (ATTACK / RELATE one-liners)
- **Stories** → daily DM funnel + poll engagement
- **Giveaway-reels** (subset of 15) → comments + followers spike

## EXECUTION NOTES FOR PURAN

1. **Product slot is a guidance, not a lock.** On execution day, pick the in-stock SKU that best fits the angle. If the suggested category is out, swap to any SKU in the same brand that carries the same narrative role.
2. **Reels must be planned 7 days ahead.** Each one needs GPT stills → Grok Aurora animation → CapCut assembly. Studio gives stills + Grok prompts when the slot is briefed.
3. **Giveaway-reels need a ManyChat asset prepped before posting.** Biomart Jul 11 needs the pantry-list DM ready by Jul 9. Caveman Jul 27 needs the Sawan snack guide ready by Jul 25.
4. **Story drumbeat is daily — 8 AM + 8 PM IST.** Not slot-counted above; treat as standard.
5. **English-only captions on Reels.** Hinglish allowed on posts/stories where brand voice fits (Caveman, Biomart).
6. **No offer/price/code in any output unless explicitly briefed in session.**
7. **Hashtag whitespace**: tag #HariyaliTeej #NagPanchami #Sawan2026 #WorldNatureConservationDay #GuruPurnima #InternationalTigerDay #WorldChocolateDay on the relevant date — competitors are barely using these.

## WHY THIS CALENDAR WILL OUTPERFORM JUNE

- **Reach engine:** 15 reels concentrated on festival/cultural peaks → ride existing trend tags instead of fighting for cold reach
- **Save engine:** EDUCATE-carousel cadence (~10 across the month) → builds the trust corpus competitors are already winning on
- **Comment engine:** 2 well-placed giveaway-reels + 6 RELATE-monologue reels → mirrors the proven 5K-comment / 1M-view formula from competitor data
- **Sales engine:** CONVERT slots stack into W4 + W5 (Teej → Sawan begin) where buying intent is naturally highest
- **Follower engine:** Conservation Day + Guru Purnima + Tiger Day cluster (Jul 28–29) = three "good-for-the-planet" hooks back-to-back → ride the share wave
- **Flex:** angle-led structure means OOS doesn't break the calendar — Puran swaps SKUs without rebuilding the strategic intent
