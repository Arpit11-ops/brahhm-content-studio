## THE FORMULA

Every greendipz reel follows this structure. No exceptions.

### Format
- **Length:** 15-25 seconds
- **Ratio:** 9:16 vertical
- **Style:** Phone-shot feel. Real kitchen. Real food. ASMR sounds audible
- **Production:** CapCut assembly. No GPT visuals. No text cards mid-reel
- **Audio:** Original cooking sounds (sizzle, pour, chop) + trending background music at 30% volume

### Structure (4 Beats)

**BEAT 1 — THE SITUATION (0-3 seconds)**
Hook frame. No product visible. Show the MOMENT, not the brand.
"Weeknight. Tired. Want Manchurian."
Visual: Empty pan on stove. Or fridge opening. Or tired hands on counter.

**BEAT 2 — THE COOK (3-12 seconds)**
Real food being cooked with greendipz sauce. Close-up ASMR.
Sauce being poured from jar (jar visible, label readable). Food hitting pan.
Sizzle. Steam. Toss. Quick cuts every 2-3 seconds.
Product enters HERE — naturally, as part of cooking.

**BEAT 3 — THE REVEAL (12-18 seconds)**
Finished dish plated. Overhead shot. Sauce glossy and visible.
Garnish moment. Fork/spoon lift showing texture.
This is the money shot. Hold for 2-3 seconds.

**BEAT 4 — THE JAR (18-22 seconds)**
Quick cut to jar label. Clean shot. 1-2 seconds max.
NO voiceover saying "greendipz" — the label speaks.
Cut to black or loop back to BEAT 3.

### Caption Template (Reel-specific)
40-60 words. Story-first. Product name in middle, not hook.
End with genuine question OR ManyChat trigger.
Max 3 hashtags: #greendipz + 1 cuisine + 1 food moment tag.

### Thumbnail
Use T56 — Food Close-Up style. Finished dish tight crop. Warm. Appetizing.
Consistent across all greendipz reels for grid coherence.

### What NOT To Do
- No text overlays during the cooking (CapCut auto-captions OK for voiceover)
- No product-first opening. Product enters at BEAT 2, never BEAT 1
- No multiple dishes in one reel. One sauce. One dish. One story
- No Hinglish in reel captions — English only per Protocol 9
- No static frames. Every second has motion — pour, sizzle, toss, plate

### Content Calendar: greendipz Reel Rotation
100% reels. 3-4 per week. Rotate across:
- **Monday:** Indo-Chinese (Schezwan, Manchurian, Kung Pao)
- **Wednesday:** Indian Gravies (Butter Chicken, Paneer Makhani, Biryani)
- **Friday:** Italian/Mexican (Arrabbiata, Salsa Mexicana)
- **Saturday (optional):** "Which sauce wins?" comparison or leftover hack
