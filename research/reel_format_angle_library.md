## PART B — Angle Library (12 marketing angles)

Every reel picks ONE angle. Format is HOW; angle is WHY.

| Angle | When to use | Emotional trigger | Watched-till-end hook style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ATTACK — competitor** | Direct competitor named product, category leader | Anger + validation | "Stop paying ₹X for [product]" |
| **ATTACK — category lie** | Whole category is misleading (added sugar, fake vanilla, refined oils) | Betrayal + curiosity | "[Category] is not what you think" |
| **ATTACK — restaurant / delivery** | Home-cook can beat restaurant/delivery for less | Empowerment | "Cancel the ₹450 [dish] order" |
| **EDUCATE — decode** | Ingredient list explained, label decoded | Insider knowledge | "Most Indians don't know [surprising fact]" |
| **EDUCATE — comparison** | 2+ options ranked with reasoning | Decision-making anxiety | "[A] vs [B] — which actually works for [audience]" |
| **EDUCATE — how-to** | Recipe, technique, hack | Competence gain | "[Outcome] in [short time], no [pain]" |
| **RELATE — mom / parent** | Parent audience, daily struggle relatable | Recognition + relief | "If your kid says 'I'm bored' 10x a day, this" |
| **RELATE — regional / cultural** | India-specific food memory, ritual, festival | Belonging | "Dadi ki recipe vs YouTube recipe" |
| **RELATE — self-deprecating humour** | Founder or creator laughing at themselves | Warmth | "Not to flex but I'm officially chef approved 💅🏽" |
| **CONVERT — price-per-serving math** | Concrete rupee value shown | Rational payoff | "₹310 = 4 restaurant plates" |
| **CONVERT — proof / factory tour** | Behind-the-scenes at facility | Trust | Nithya's "This didn't feel like a factory visit" pattern — 142K views |
| **CONVERT — social proof stack** | Creator/UGC + brand together | Herd validation | "10/10 recommend this LIFE HACK" — Teesh's Veeba reel |

## PART C — Live scrape observations (what's actually crushing right now)

From 48 recent reels across yogabars, whole truth, slurrp farm, veeba (July 2026):

1. **Short + product-focused = massive views.** Yogabar's 20-second product reel with the fermented protein claim hit **6.06M views**. Structure: hero product + 3 spec claims + 1 line CTA.

2. **Myth-busters win on Whole Truth.** "Vanilla flavour is not vanilla" (176K), "Are your eggs really antibiotic free?" (23K + 202 comments), "Should we be buying A2 Ghee?" (86K). Format: single question hook + 30-40s answer, founder-to-camera.

3. **Mom UGC is a scaling engine for Slurrp Farm.** ~10 of their top 20 recent reels are creator-partnerships with mom accounts (`@themombooth`, `@ohmother.in`, `@bgandtheboys`, `@alka_dickson`). Same template: kitchen chaos → "Mumma, can I have something yummy?" → product opens the pack → happy kid eats. Averaging 40K–256K views per creator post.

4. **Government / partnership content unexpectedly viral.** Yogabar's MyGov podcast clip hit **2.58M views**. Legitimacy signal that money can't buy.

5. **Veeba wins on aesthetic + humour.** "Team Ketchup or Team Mustard" (264K), "6-course supper" (269K), watermelon Caesar salad (289K). Bold food editorial + confident brand voice. Most Veeba reels are 20-30 seconds.

6. **Longer reels only work with a genuinely deep story.** Whole Truth's "why real artisanal food at scale is tough" (61K, 110s) works because the vulnerability earns the length. Everything else >60s under-performs.

7. **Zero hashtags on top-performing brand reels.** Whole Truth and yogabars.in native posts run with 0 hashtags on their biggest wins. UGC/creator posts still use 3-5.

## PART E — Reels that are OFF-LIMITS for us

Skip these formats — brand fit is wrong:

- **POV: Me in My 90s** — needs a strong on-camera creator personality. Not our lane yet.
- **Half-Hair Flip** — personal-care native, doesn't map to organic food.
- **Trending audio rides** — high-risk, high-reward. Only when a specific audio genuinely fits.
- **Jhinka Chika audio trend** — coffee/energy-drink specific.
