The hook uses loss aversion: viewers immediately recognise the pain of an inflated restaurant bill. The reel then gives them relief through a familiar home-cooking sequence.

The split-screen creates contrast in the first 2 seconds, which supports retention. The cooking sequence keeps visual change under 2.5 seconds, and the reveal gives a save/share reason: this is a Friday dinner idea, not just a product shot.

The product enters after the problem, matching greendipz's reel formula and avoiding a product-first open.
