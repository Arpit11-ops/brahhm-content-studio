## CapCut finishing checklist (after Flow returns all 6 clips)

1. Import 6 clips in order (Beat 1 → 6) on one 9:16 timeline.
2. Trim each to exactly 3 seconds if Flow generated longer clips; keep the first 3s of each (motion completes in the first 3s per specs above).
3. Cut boundaries between beats are HARD CUTS — no cross-fade, no dissolve.
4. VO check: play through, confirm each line is clearly audible and dominant over the music bed. If any VO is muddy, re-generate that beat in Flow or add a voiceover pass in CapCut.
5. Music bed: if Flow's per-clip lo-fi bed jumps between beats, replace with a single continuous lo-fi track under the whole reel, ducked automatically under VO.
6. Add Caveman logo watermark, lower-right corner, 8% opacity, off-white, tiny.
7. Export 1080×1920, MP4, H.264, high bitrate.
