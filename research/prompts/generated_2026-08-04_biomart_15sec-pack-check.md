## Variation

Every organic pack has five tells. The certification is a real logo, not the word "natural" in a script font. The method — cold pressed, stone ground, wood churned — is printed on the front because they're proud of it. The ingredient list is one line long. The FSSAI licence is real and looks it up. And the label bothers to tell you what it's free from. Miss any of five and the pack is telling you something. Our sesame oil ticks all five, so does the mustard sitting next to it on the shelf.
