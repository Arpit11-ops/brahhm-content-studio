## 12. WHY IT WILL PERFORM

- **Turmeric is the most visually cinematic spice in the Indian pantry** — the field-to-powder arc gives every beat a hard colour shift (green field → red soil → orange root → gold powder), which Agent Mode reads cleanly and stitches without morph artifacts.
- **Adulteration anxiety is a live conversation** — Indian consumers are actively worried about lead and chalk in commodity haldi; "one ingredient on the label, because that is the whole recipe" enters that anxiety without making a claim we can't prove.
- **Grandmother-recognition hook** — activates identity + nostalgia without going Hinglish; keeps the Reel caption rule intact.
- **18-second, five-beat pacing** — matches Protocol 8 v4.1's honoured length range for Agent Mode; low compression risk on the CTA beat.
- **Product reveal on beat 4** — protects mid-reel drop-off, lets the field-and-root story earn the pack.
- **Bundle pull to black pepper in caption body** — natural haldi-kali mirch pairing, AOV upgrade without a DM flow (Protocol 10 compliant).
