## 12. WHY IT WILL PERFORM

- **Format lift**: Yogabars archetype cut is currently the highest-save reel format in the Indian organic category (Jul 2026 scrape). Six quick person-in-pantry cuts hit six identification triggers in 15 seconds. Viewers self-recognise at least one archetype → save + share to a friend who fits another.
- **Curation identity, not product ad**: Biomart's own weakness has been feeling like a warehouse. This reel positions it as *the shelf every kind of Indian household already lives on* — softer, more human, more repeat-viewable than any single-SKU ad.
- **Colour world binding**: 6 unrelated scenes held by one warm cream + green + gold palette. That palette IS the Biomart signature — first time we're teaching the feed to recognise it across contexts.
- **VO-first structure**: no baked archetype titles means the reel doesn't feel like it's telling you what to be. It shows lives and lets you locate yourself. Higher completion rate than title-labelled cuts (Yogabars data).
- **Bracket hook**: "Six pantries. One shop." opens (VO) and closes (VO + text card) — the phrase is the reel's own hashtag. Increases the odds of it getting captioned in shares/reposts.
- **Cross-brand demonstration**: 4 sibling brands appear organically without a "we own all these" slide. Subtle equity build for the parent company.

None — this reel IS the bundle. Every archetype's pantry is a soft-mention of a mini-bundle available on biomart.in. The caption line "every cookie, every seed, every spice, every sauce" does the cross-sell work without a pushy pairing line.
