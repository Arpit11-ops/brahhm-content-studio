## 8. CAPTION

Read the label. Not the marketing.

"Wood-pressed" is not a certification. It has no legal definition in India, no accredited body checking it, and no lab test to verify it. Any brand can print those two words on a bottle and no one will stop them.

"Cold-pressed" means something specific: seeds pressed below 40°C so the natural aroma, colour, and nutrients survive the extraction. Pusht prints it on the label because Pusht actually does it — the same kachi ghani principle our grandmothers trusted, done at PGS-India Organic certified standard, since 2003.

Next time you pick up any oil bottle, look for two things on the label: the words "cold pressed" and a real organic certification mark. Not a slogan. A stamp.
