**Why 3 × 5s and not 1 × 15s:**
- Flow's hard maximum per clip is 6 seconds. Anything longer than 6s in a single prompt = Flow auto-splits it or compresses ugly. That's the HF-Ganesh failure mode.
- The fix isn't shortening the reel — it's **intentionally planning the split** as three separate 5s Flow renders (1s under the 6s cap, safety headroom) that we designed to hard-cut cleanly at seams.
- Hard cuts at planned seams = film-grammar clean. Auto-splits at unplanned points = artifact-y. Same seam length, opposite intent.
- Each clip is fully self-contained — its own preservation clause, its own beat, its own VO line. Flow never has to reason across a 15s arc; it just renders each 5s beat in isolation and CapCut assembles.
- CapCut stitch: three straight cuts, no dissolves, no transitions. Then +2s freeze on the final hero frame for the CTA end-card.
