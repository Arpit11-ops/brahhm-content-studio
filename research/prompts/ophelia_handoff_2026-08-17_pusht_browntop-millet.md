## 1. Brand Brief

**Brand:** Pusht Organic (Instagram: @pushtorganic · site: pusht.in)
**Product line:** Certified organic Indian pantry staples — millets, dals, rice, cold-pressed oils, spices. Farm-to-pack provenance is the whole story.
**This SKU:** Organic Browntop Millet (Makra) — one of India's rarest heritage millets, revived by a small cohort of certified organic farmers in Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka. Naturally gluten-free, easy to digest, mild earthy flavour, cooks like rice. 1kg ₹390 / 500g ₹195.

**Positioning & tone**
Honest farm editorial. Warm, unhurried, documentary. National Geographic meets a well-lit Indian kitchen — never rustic-cliché, never wellness-slogan. English voice with a natural Indian cadence. The brand talks like a farmer's grandchild who happens to also be a good writer: specific, quiet, proud without shouting.

**Colour palette**
- Forest Green — `#143C28` (primary brand ink)
- Warm Cream — `#F5EFDF` (backdrop / negative space)
- Honey Amber — `#D6A24A` (golden-hour warmth, grain colour)
- Wood Brown — `#6B4A2A` (weathered counter, secondary warmth)

**Tagline for this reel:** *The grain India forgot.*

**Logo & pack do's**
- Front of pack only. Upright. Zero tilt. Front-facing to camera.
- Every printed pack element (logo, product name, illustration, colour blocks, forest-green + warm-cream typography) must reproduce sharp and unwarped from the reference photo.
- Warm rim-light on the pack edge is on-brand.

**Logo & pack don'ts**
- No back-of-pack. No side or three-quarter tilt in this reel.
- No redesigning or restyling pack graphics. No added text on the pack itself.
- No rustic-cliché props anywhere in frame: no wooden bowls, no burlap, no jute weave, no mortar-and-pestle.
- No full stops on any headline, caption, or CTA card. Middot `·` allowed as a separator.
- No exclamation marks, no "click / tap / swipe / link in bio" language.
- No discounts, prices, or offer codes anywhere.
