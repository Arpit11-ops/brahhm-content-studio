8-slide carousel. Cover slide shows the full 6-pack shelf as a 2×3 grid with the hook baked in. **Cover + close slides:** 6 packs in 2 rows of 3, centered, each pack ≥28% frame width ([[feedback_gpt_pack_row_geometry]]).
