## 8. CAPTION

Cold-pressed and raw-pressed are not the same oil in a different bottle.

Cold-pressed groundnut oil comes out of a slow wooden ghani that never heats up. The peanut keeps its aroma, the colour stays deep golden, nothing has to be bleached out later.

Raw-pressed is a faster steel crush. The friction alone runs hot, and by the time it reaches your bottle, most of the nutty smell you paid for is gone.

If your tadka doesn't smell like the peanut, the oil lost the fight before it hit the pan.

Try it in a hot arhar dal tadka once. You'll stop switching back.
