# Brand Bible Updates — June 2026
## Research-Validated Changes for All 5 Brand Bibles

All changes below are based on competitor research (10 accounts, 120 posts,
198 hashtag posts) and Instagram algorithm 2026 intelligence.

---

## CAVEMAN ORGANIC — Brand Bible Update

### Content Angle Ratio (NEW)
**OLD:** 36% ATTACK / 57% CONVERT / 7% RELATE / 0% EDUCATE
**NEW:** 35% RELATE / 30% EDUCATE / 25% ATTACK / 10% CONVERT
**WHY:** Caveman's 8% profile-to-follow conversion is directly caused by a grid dominated by sales content. Instagram 2026 algorithmically suppresses CONVERT content from accounts under 5K followers. Complete inversion required.

### Content Format (NEW)
**OLD:** Primarily static GPT-generated posts
**NEW:** 75% reels / 25% static
**WHY:** Reels drive 19x more engagement than images. Every competitor above 100K followers runs 67-92% reels.

### Reel Strategy (NEW — add as new section)
- Creator/influencer partnership reels (real people eating Caveman products)
- Post-gym snacking moments (phone-shot, real kitchen/gym bag)
- Snack comparison reels (Caveman vs conventional — side by side)
- Reference: @yogabars.in format (14.4M views with creator everyday moments)

### Grid Coherence (NEW)
Maximum 2 visual styles on grid:
1. Dark moody product shots (GPT Image 2.0 — T6, T41 family)
2. Real person snacking moments (reel thumbnails — T57)

### Thumbnail Style
T57 — Person + Product. Dark background. Consistent across all reels.

### Tone Adjustment
No change to core tone (bold, urgent, blunt). But APPLY the tone to RELATE and EDUCATE content, not just ATTACK. Caveman being blunt about everyday struggles (4pm hunger, gym bag emptiness) is on-brand AND drives shares.

## HEALTH FIELDS — Brand Bible Update

### Content Angle Ratio (ADJUSTMENT)
**OLD:** 65% EDUCATE / 24% RELATE / 6% ATTACK / 6% CONVERT
**NEW:** 40% RELATE / 30% EDUCATE / 20% ATTACK / 10% CONVERT
**WHY:** EDUCATE is strong but RELATE is what drove the best performer (Chamomile reel 16.4K views). The 157% profile-to-follow conversion from the Chamomile reel proves wellness lifestyle content drives followers directly.

### Content Format (NEW)
**OLD:** Mixed static + reels
**NEW:** 70% reels / 30% static (carousels for education still viable)
**WHY:** Chamomile reel proved the format. Scale it.

### Reel Strategy (NEW — add as new section)
- Morning wellness routine reels (real person, real kitchen, warm light)
- Founder/heritage story reels (Since 2003 — real person telling the story)
- Tea preparation ASMR reels (close-up brewing, pouring, steam)
- Reference: @vahdamindia format (27K views with "women in your 40s" wellness)
- Reference: @organicindiaofficial (290K views with lifestyle aspiration)

### Grid Coherence (NEW)
Maximum 2 visual styles on grid:
1. Teal lifestyle wellness moments (reel thumbnails — T58)
2. Premium product hero shots (GPT Image 2.0 — T2, T48 family)

### Thumbnail Style
T58 — Wellness Moment. Warm light. Cup/product in hands. Teal undertone.

### Tone Adjustment
Slight warmth increase for RELATE content. Health Fields can be authoritative AND warm when talking about morning rituals, family wellness, daily habits. The Rozana sub-brand tone should expand to more of the account — not just Rozana products but the daily ritual angle.

No change to core tone (fun, punchy, flavour-first). But the EXECUTION shifts entirely to reel format. The tone stays the same; the medium changes completely.

## BIOMART — Brand Bible Update

### Content Identity (CRITICAL — NEW SECTION)
See: research/biomart_content_identity.md
**Identity:** The Curation Voice — the person who picked everything on the shelf.
**Positioning:** "I tested 50 organic honeys. These 3 made the cut."

### Content Angle Ratio (NEW)
**NEW:** 35% EDUCATE / 30% RELATE / 25% ATTACK / 10% CONVERT
**WHY:** Curation IS education. Combined with founder personality (RELATE), this builds an identity.

### Content Pillars (REPLACE existing)
1. **Curation (45%):** "I tested X products. These Y made the cut." — reel format
2. **Haul/Bundle (30%):** "This week's organic haul — one order." — reel format
3. **Behind the Verification (25%):** "How we decide what makes it to biomart.in" — reel format

### Content Format (NEW)
**NEW:** 80% reels / 20% static
**WHY:** 93.3% of views already from reels. Biomart needs discovery more than any brand.

### Grid Coherence (NEW)
Maximum 2 visual styles on grid:
1. Curated product spreads (reel thumbnails — T60)
2. Founder/team face moments (real photos, not GPT)

### STOP IMMEDIATELY
1. Reposting content from other brand accounts
2. Generic multi-brand festival posts
3. Product catalog posts with no editorial voice
4. The Eid Mubarak post — archive from grid

### Tone Adjustment
Move from "organised shopping companion" to "knowledgeable friend who runs an organic store." The difference: friends have opinions, companions follow instructions.

## CROSS-BRAND UPDATES (apply to all Brand Bibles)

### Hashtag Count
**OLD:** 5 hashtags per post
**NEW:** Maximum 3 hashtags per post
**WHY:** Research shows posts without hashtags achieved 23% higher reach in 2026

### Reel Caption Length
**NEW RULE:** Reel captions: 40-80 words. Static captions: 80-150 words.
The video does the work. The caption supports.

### Question Test
**NEW RULE (June 1-14):** End captions with a genuine specific question instead of only a ManyChat trigger. Track comment rate. If comments increase >20%, adopt as default.

### Face In Frame
**NEW RULE:** Reels with a face in frame get 35% higher retention. Every brand should have face-visible content at least 40% of the time.
