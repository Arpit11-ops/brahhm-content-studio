## THE FORMULA

Every Pusht reel follows this structure. Street food nostalgia meets certified organic.

### Format
- **Length:** 20-30 seconds
- **Ratio:** 9:16 vertical
- **Style:** Golden hour warmth. Real Indian kitchen. Hands in frame
- **Production:** CapCut assembly. Original cooking sounds. Voiceover in Hinglish
- **Audio:** Original voiceover (Hinglish) + cooking ASMR + soft trending music at 20%

### Structure (5 Beats)

**BEAT 1 — THE MEMORY (0-4 seconds)**
Hook frame. Nostalgia trigger. No product visible yet.
"Teri dadi ke ghar ki dal alag kyun lagti thi?"
Visual: Hands opening a jar. Or a gas stove clicking on. Or ingredients being laid out on a kitchen counter. Warm golden light.

**BEAT 2 — THE INGREDIENTS (4-8 seconds)**
Show the raw Pusht product being used — dal being measured, oil being poured, millet being rinsed. Label visible but not forced.
Voiceover connects ingredient to memory.
"Woh tel wala smell yaad hai? Cold-pressed hota tha. Pusht waala bhi."

**BEAT 3 — THE COOK (8-18 seconds)**
Real cooking. Tadka sizzling. Dal bubbling. Poha being tossed.
Close-up ASMR. Quick cuts. Hands always in frame.
This is the longest beat — the cooking IS the content.

**BEAT 4 — THE SERVE (18-24 seconds)**
Finished dish being served into a steel katori or thali.
Overhead shot of the complete plate. Garnish with fresh coriander.
Hold 2-3 seconds. This is the emotional payoff.

**BEAT 5 — THE LABEL (24-28 seconds)**
Quick cut to Pusht product packaging. Clean, well-lit.
Voiceover: "Pusht Organic. Certified."
1-2 seconds. No hard sell.

### Caption Template (Reel-specific)
50-70 words. Hinglish default. Nostalgic opening, certified closing.
End with genuine question about food memories.
Max 3 hashtags: #PushtOrganic + 1 food tag + 1 cooking tag.

### Thumbnail
Use T59 — Golden Food Moment style. Hands in frame. Warm golden tones.
Steel utensils. Indian kitchen energy. Consistent across all Pusht reels.

### What NOT To Do
- No English-only captions for Pusht reels — Hinglish is the voice
- No GPT-generated food images — real food only
- No multiple products in one reel. One product. One dish. One memory
- No product-first opening. Memory comes first, product supports
- No slick production. This should feel like your mom's kitchen, not a studio

### Content Calendar: Pusht Reel Rotation

- **Monday:** Street Food Revival (chaat, poha, upma with Pusht ingredients)
- **Thursday:** Dal Story (different dal each week with farmer connection)

## WHY THIS WORKS (Algorithm Logic)

1. **Completion rate:** Nostalgia hooks hold attention. People watch to see the finished memory
2. **DM shares:** "Maa ko bhejo yeh" — family food content triggers forward behavior
3. **Saves:** Recipe format gets saved. Practical + emotional
4. **Identity:** Pusht at 404 followers needs an identity, not just products. Street food nostalgia IS the identity
5. **Hinglish:** Matches the audience. Organic buyers in India think in Hindi, browse in English. Hinglish bridges both
