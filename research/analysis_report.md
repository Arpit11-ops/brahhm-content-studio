## 1. THE ALGORITHM IN 2026 — WHAT ACTUALLY DRIVES REACH

### Ranking Signals (Priority Order)
1. **Watch time / completion rate** — most important signal. 50% of viewers drop off within first 3 seconds
2. **Shares via DM** — primary distribution trigger for non-follower reach (the reach that grows your account)
3. **Saves** — strongest "content is valuable" signal
4. **Likes per reach ratio** — not raw likes, the ratio matters
5. **Comments** — weighted, but less than shares and saves

### Critical Benchmarks
- Reels get **2.25x more reach** than static posts
- **55% of reel views** come from non-followers — reels are your discovery engine
- Accounts under 5K followers: **3.79% average engagement rate** (this is where all 5 Brahhm brands sit)
- **Posts without hashtags achieved 23% higher reach** than hashtag-heavy posts — massive shift from previous years
- Face in frame increases retention by **35%**

### What Gets Penalised
- Cross-platform watermarks (TikTok, CapCut logos)
- Recycled/repurposed content detected by "Originality Score"
- Over-posting: 10+ reels/week yields diminishing returns
- Over-produced content that doesn't feel native to Instagram

### Optimal Content Mix (Algorithm-Validated)
- 40% value/educational
- 25% relatable/trending
- 20% behind-the-scenes
- 15% promotional

### Recommended Posting Frequency
3-5 reels per week. Quality over volume.

## 5. CAPTION STRUCTURE PATTERNS

### Structure of Top 10 Captions
1. **Hook line** — emotion or situation, under 10 words (appears before "Read More")
2. **Story expansion** — 2-3 sentences expanding the moment, building relatability
3. **Product weave** — product enters naturally as the solution, not announced
4. **Soft CTA or community question** — "What's your moment?" or simple tag
5. **Hashtags** — 3-5 max. Mixed branded + discovery. NOT hashtag walls

### Key Caption Observations
- Zero top-performing captions use bullets
- Zero use emojis as section breaks
- The top performers read like a friend's story, not a marketing brief
- Product mentions come in the middle or end, never the hook
- CTAs are conversational questions, not "Comment KEYWORD"
- Top performers average 80-150 words per caption — not short, not long

## 6. VISUAL PATTERNS — WHAT TOP REELS LOOK LIKE

### Dominant Visual Styles
1. **Phone-shot UGC aesthetic** — handheld, natural light, real kitchen/room, no studio
2. **Close-up food ASMR** — tight shots of cooking, stirring, plating, crunching sounds
3. **Person-to-camera talking** — face visible, natural setting, casual outfit
4. **Cinematic lifestyle b-roll** — golden hour, slow motion food pour, aspirational but warm
5. **Quick-cut recipe montage** — overhead + side angles, fast pacing, satisfying reveal

### What's NOT in Top Performers
- No heavy GPT-generated imagery in top 20 viral reels
- No text-heavy graphic cards
- No product-on-gradient studio shots
- No ingredient explosion composites
- No template-driven carousel graphics with feature chips

### The Tension Resolved
The data confirms what your May audit showed: **authentic food moments with minimal production consistently outperform the production-heavy system.** Every single reel above 100K views is either real food being cooked, a real person talking, or both. Zero are AI-generated visuals.

**However** — this does NOT mean GPT Image 2.0 is dead. It means:
- GPT imagery serves STATIC posts and story frames, not reels
- Reels must be real food, real people, real moments
- The production system's role shifts from "hero content" to "brand world consistency on the grid"
- Grid consistency still matters for profile-to-follow conversion

## 9. HASHTAG STRATEGY UPDATE

### Research Finding
Posts without hashtags achieved **23% higher reach** in 2026 vs hashtag-heavy posts. Instagram's discovery engine now relies on content signals (watch time, shares, saves), not hashtags.

### Recommendation
- Drop from 5 hashtags to **3 maximum**
- 1 branded + 1 category + 1 discovery
- Never more than 3
- Test 0 hashtags on reels (let the algorithm classify)
