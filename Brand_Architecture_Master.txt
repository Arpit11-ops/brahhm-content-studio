BRAND CONFUSION PREVENTION — 5 MOST COMMON TONE ERRORS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ERROR 1: Writing Pusht in Caveman's voice.
  WRONG:  "Stop eating dal that's lying to you. Pusht is the primal choice."
  RIGHT:  "Teri dadi ke ghar ki dal alag kyun lagti thi? Kyunki woh
           chemical-free thi. Pusht brings that back."

ERROR 2: Writing Health Fields in Pusht's warm/nostalgic tone.
  WRONG:  "Feel the warmth of nature's farm in every sip of our honey."
  RIGHT:  "Cold-extracted. Lab-verified. APEDA-certified. Rozana Forest
           Honey — 20 years of the same standard."

ERROR 3: Writing Biomart posts without the code and delivery threshold.
  WRONG:  "Shop our full organic range today!"
  RIGHT:  "Shop the full range at biomart.in — use code BIOMART10 for
           10% off. Free delivery above ₹1,599."

ERROR 4: Writing greendipz as "Greendipz" or leading with health claims.
  WRONG:  "Greendipz Schezwan Sauce is a healthy alternative to..."
  RIGHT:  "greendipz Schezwan. Fiery. Smoky. Real heat. No E-numbers."

ERROR 5: Writing Caveman content in a soft, wellness-brand tone.
  WRONG:  "Nourish your body with the gentle goodness of organic millets..."
  RIGHT:  "Kodo millet. Real butter. Zero palm oil. That's your snack now."
